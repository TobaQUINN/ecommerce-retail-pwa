# Business Context

## Overview

Modern Retail Platform is a digital storefront for a family-owned retail business located in Ijoko, Ogun State, Nigeria.

The business primarily serves customers in Ogun State and Lagos State through physical retail, online inquiries and direct orders.

The website extends the physical store rather than replacing it.

## Business Goals

- Increase product visibility.
- Provide customers with a modern shopping experience.
- Reduce repetitive customer inquiries.
- Allow customers to browse products before visiting the store.
- Simplify order requests and order management.
- Build trust through a professional online presence.

## Departments

### Electronics & Gadgets

Products include but are not limited to:

- Chargers
- Earbuds
- Phone Accessories
- Smart Watches
- Bluetooth Speakers
- Gaming Consoles
- Controllers
- Power Banks
- Other electronic gadgets

### Fashion & Lifestyle

Products include but are not limited to:

- Clothing
- Shoes
- Jewelry
- Underwear
- Perfumes
- Fashion Accessories

## Target Audience

Primary customers include:

- Residents of Ogun State.
- Residents of Lagos State.
- Customers searching online before visiting the physical store.
- Customers who prefer ordering remotely.
- Returning customers looking for new arrivals.

Most visitors are expected to use mobile devices with varying internet speeds.

## Customer Expectations

Customers should be able to:

- Browse products easily.
- Search products quickly.
- View accurate product information.
- Check estimated availability.
- Request an order.
- Contact the business.
- Visit the physical store if preferred.

The experience should remain simple even for first-time online shoppers.

## Purchasing Philosophy

The website supports two purchasing methods.

### In-Store Purchase

Customers may browse the catalogue online and visit the store to inspect and purchase products physically.

The website should actively encourage this option for nearby customers.

### Online Order Request

Customers submit an order request.

The business verifies:

- Product availability
- Quantity
- Delivery feasibility
- Delivery cost

Only after verification are payment instructions provided.

This approach prioritizes trust and reduces misunderstandings.

## Business Values

Every interaction should communicate:

- Honesty
- Transparency
- Reliability
- Professionalism
- Customer-first service

The platform should never pressure customers into making immediate payments.

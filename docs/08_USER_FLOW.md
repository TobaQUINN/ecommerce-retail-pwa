# User Flow

## Goal

Provide customers with a simple, trustworthy and frictionless shopping experience.

Customers should never feel confused about what to do next.

---

## Primary User Journey

Landing Page

↓

Choose Department

↓

Browse Categories

↓

Search or Filter Products

↓

View Product Details

↓

Add Product to Cart

↓

Review Cart

↓

Checkout

↓

Submit Order Request

↓

Order Verification

↓

Receive Confirmation

↓

Make Payment

↓

Upload Payment Proof

↓

Payment Verification

↓

Order Processing

↓

Delivery or Store Pickup

↓

Order Completed

---

## In-Store Customer Journey

Landing Page

↓

Browse Products

↓

Check Availability

↓

View Store Information

↓

Visit Physical Store

↓

Inspect Product

↓

Purchase In-Person

The website should actively support customers who simply want to know what is available before visiting.

---

## Checkout Journey

Customer provides:

- Full Name
- Phone Number
- Delivery Address
- State
- Local Government
- Order Notes (Optional)

Customer submits an Order Request.

Payment is NOT requested immediately.

---

## Order Verification

Before requesting payment, the business verifies:

- Product availability
- Quantity
- Delivery feasibility
- Delivery cost
- Pickup or delivery method

Only verified orders proceed to payment.

---

## Payment Journey

Verified Order

↓

Customer receives payment instructions

↓

Customer transfers payment

↓

Uploads payment proof

↓

Business verifies payment

↓

Order Processing

---

## Order Completion

Customer receives updates as the order progresses.

Possible statuses include:

- Pending Verification
- Ready for Payment
- Payment Submitted
- Payment Confirmed
- Processing
- Ready for Pickup
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered
- Cancelled

The customer should always know the current status of their order.

---

## UX Principles

Every user journey should be:

- Simple
- Predictable
- Mobile-friendly
- Transparent
- Trustworthy

Never leave users wondering what happens next.

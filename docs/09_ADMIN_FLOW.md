# Admin Flow

## Goal

Provide administrators with a simple and efficient interface for managing daily business operations.

---

## Administrator Journey

Login

↓

Dashboard

↓

View Orders

↓

Verify Order

↓

Confirm Availability

↓

Estimate Delivery

↓

Approve Order

↓

Receive Payment Proof

↓

Verify Payment

↓

Process Order

↓

Mark Ready

↓

Deliver or Prepare Pickup

↓

Complete Order

---

## Dashboard

The dashboard should provide quick access to:

- Orders
- Products
- Categories
- Customers
- Notifications
- Analytics
- Settings

---

## Product Management

Administrators should be able to:

- Add Products
- Edit Products
- Delete Products
- Upload Images
- Update Prices
- Update Stock
- Assign Categories
- Mark Featured Products

---

## Order Management

Administrators should be able to:

- View New Orders
- Verify Availability
- Calculate Delivery Fee
- Contact Customer
- Approve Orders
- Cancel Orders
- Update Order Status

---

## Payment Verification

Administrators should:

Review uploaded payment proof.

Confirm payment manually.

Reject invalid payments.

Record verification details.

Update payment status.

---

## Customer Management

Administrators should be able to:

- View Customer Information
- View Order History
- Contact Customers

---

## Inventory Management

Administrators should update:

- Product Availability
- Stock Quantity
- Product Visibility

Inventory information should remain accurate.

---

## Notifications

Administrators receive notifications for:

- New Order Requests
- Payment Submitted
- Low Stock
- Customer Messages

---

## Security

Only authenticated administrators may access the dashboard.

Permissions should support future role-based access.

---

## Admin Experience

The dashboard should prioritize:

- Speed
- Simplicity
- Readability
- Efficient workflows

Administrators should accomplish common tasks with minimal clicks.
